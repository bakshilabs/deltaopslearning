      In the full lab

      Each lab has six stages with three cards at each, giving 729 possible routes rather than one “correct” answer, and every card leads to a different result that children can compare and explain.

      About this demo

      This demonstration runs on our lab engine, where the helper uses prepared responses rather than a live AI model and the lab tells the facilitator when it is a good moment to pause and ask a question.

      Why organisations choose it

      Designed for children and easy for staff to run.

      • Safe to use

        Children choose from prepared cards whose responses have all been written and tested in advance, so there is no open chatbot, no free typing and no child account.

      • Easy to lead

        Your staff run the session as they normally would, and they need no specialist AI knowledge because the lab prompts them with the next question to ask.

      • Made for your setting

        Your subject, your audience and your learning goals shape the story, the missions and what children make.

      We are developing these labs with partners, and before each pilot we agree the session plan, safeguarding, data handling and technical set-up together.

      How we work

      We build it with you, try it with one group, then decide together what comes next.

      1. 1

        Design together

        We plan one experience around your group, setting and learning goals.

      2. 2

        Pilot

        Your staff run it with a first group, then we review together what worked and what needs changing.

      3. 3

        Expand

        We improve the lab, then support you to use it more widely if the pilot shows it is worthwhile.
